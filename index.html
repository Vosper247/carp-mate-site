Carp Mate

Your mate on the bank.

  • When they’ll feed. The day’s feeding times from the sun and moon, and a score for the conditions where you are: pressure, wind, cloud, temperature and the moon.
  • Your log. Sessions, catches, weights, baits and rigs, with photos, and the waters and swims you fish marked on the map.
  • Works at the lake. Everything is on the phone, so no signal is no problem; your account keeps it safe when you’re back in range.

Carp Mate is in testing and isn’t in the App Store or Google Play yet.
