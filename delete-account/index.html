Delete your Carp Mate account

For the Carp Mate app, by Carp Mate App. It removes your account and everything in it.

In the app

  1. Open Carp Mate and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It needs signal, it happens straight away, and it can’t be undone. If you want a copy of your records, use Settings → Export my data first.

Without the app

Email support@carpmateapp.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will delete it and confirm by reply within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What is deleted

Your email address and password, every session, catch, water, peg and spot, your notes and your photos. They are removed from our storage straight away, and nothing is kept in a backup.

What is not deleted

Crash reports stay with Sentry for up to 30 days on our current plan, then are deleted. They hold nothing about you and aren’t tied to your account. Any backup file you exported is yours and stays wherever you put it.
